Intro

Welcome to RedGrayFox Studio.

RedGrayFox Studio is an independent video game development studio focused on creating functional, engaging, and accessible games for players worldwide.

Our Mission & Philosophy
Our goal is to develop games with clear mechanics, structured gameplay, and solid design choices. We aim to offer alternatives to large-scale commercial games by prioritizing original concepts, balanced challenges, and well-designed user experiences.

What Drives Us

Player-Centric Design: We build games around clear rules and intuitive user interfaces.
Creative Independence: We explore unique ideas and mechanics without constraints from external publishers.
Quality & Polish: We focus on refining core gameplay systems to deliver functional and rewarding experiences.

Whether you play casually or seek a new challenge, we invite you to explore our projects.

Our Videogames.

Bows of Light, a personal crafted tale.

There was a time, many years ago, when I tried to program web pages, but I gave up. I didn't understand or see things with the right perspective, and I simply abandoned it without appreciating the journey. Years later, God changed my life with the most beautiful gift: the arrival of my daughters.

They rekindled that spark inside me. A deep desire was born to create something unique for them, giving life to Bows of Light, our heartfelt 2D adventure inspired by their own antics and special moments. Although it started as a very intimate family project completed just to stay at home, it became the seed and foundational work of what is now RedGrayFox Studio.

However, the studio doesn't stop at a single horizon. At RedGrayFox Studio, we combine our passion for video games with the comprehensive development of web pages and APKs, demonstrating technical and creative versatility in every format.

And now, this journey evolves with our next big project: Collapse: A Realistic RPG, which officially stands as the studio's official opening game.

This whole path—from our most intimate beginnings to software development and new role-playing experiences—is proof that, with perseverance, faith, and purpose, past roadblocks can be overcome to build a future without limits. Thank you for being part of this journey.
